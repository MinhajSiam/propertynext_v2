Rules of Flat Purchasing with Land

The following rules and regulations govern the purchase of Flats with land under PropertyNext Ltd. All shareholders are required to read and comply with these terms in good faith.

1

Clients can book Flats according to their choice on a first-come, first-served basis.

2

The whole construction cost will be paid by all the shareholders in step-by-step as per the Joint Construction Agreement. Get Form

3

If any client is unable to pay the full price of the land share within the stipulated time, his/her Flat allotment will be canceled and the paid amount will be refunded after deducting the office charge as per the refund policy.

4

For an uninterrupted and faster construction, shareholders shall sign Joint Construction Agreement & Power of Attorney to construct the building with PropertyNext Ltd.

5

A specific percentage of the total construction cost will be paid as a service charge to PropertyNext Ltd. for implementation and supervision of construction work.

6

All other policies related to PropertyNext Ltd. are available on our official website: https://propertynext.bd/
